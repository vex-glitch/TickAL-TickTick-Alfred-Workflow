# Christina Wodtke's Radical Focus), quarterly review writers (Sarah K. Peck, Dickie Bush, Taylor Pearson and
# Dan Sullivan, Felecia Etienne, Leanna Lee's Zapier template, Get Ahead VA, Bottom Line Capital, EvyOS, Notes

## lookback
- What unexpected came up? Was there scope creep? If so, what?
- What else happened that helped the business?
- Is trying to achieve your goals negatively affecting your life in other ways?
- What's something that didn't go as planned for you but still turned out to be worth it?
- What areas did I move forward the most?
- If I had to relive one single day from the past three months over again exactly as it already played out, which day would it be? Describe it in as much detail as possible.

## lessons
- Which assumption in planning turned out to be false?
- What does your gut tell you, did you really achieve the change you were after with this OKR Set?
- Was enough time spent on the objective?
- What steps did you perform well but didn't yield desired results?
- What kind of things can you plan for, and which can you only plan to react to?
- In your work, where do you see your strengths and weaknesses?

## decisions
- What decisions did I delay that I need to revisit?
- What's the number one thing preventing your business from growing? And why aren't you fixing it?
- Were there financial decisions you are proud of or regret?
- What big thing are you putting off?
- Is there something you used to be excited about but aren't anymore?
- What 'Unfinished Business' should you nail in the first month ahead?

## direction
- Right now, are you expecting anything specific to not work out?
- What can you not afford to mess up?
- Is 12 weeks a realistic time frame for the chosen goal(s)?
- What part of your life is seriously overdue for attention?
- What opportunities need to be captured?
- What are you least clear about: in terms of strategy and goals?

## system
- What did we review every week that didn't help delivery, and what did we fail to review that mattered?
- Were stalled Key Results addressed before Week 10?
- What systems am I missing/do I need?
- What is my accountability strategy? Who or what will keep me on track?
- Can you list the things you're currently working on that aren't part of your job?
- Did you learn something about the OKR process itself?

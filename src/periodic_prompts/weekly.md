# Weekly journal pools (Vex 2026-09-24): one ## section per category, TWO prompts drawn from EVERY
# category each week (ten in all, periodic_model.select_prompts, WEEKLY_CATEGORIES). Sources: the
# Stoic app's week-scoped prompts (getstoic.com, verbatim), Vex's own list, the July pool. No chains
# (ruling: no film week). No en or em dashes.

## retrospect
- Read your last seven days of entries. What pattern shows up more than twice?
- What obstacle appeared more than once this week? Name it exactly as it kept showing up.
- Which entry from this week would you want to reread in a hard moment? Copy one line from it here.
- Which virtue got the least attention this week, based on what you actually wrote?
- Rank the four virtues, wisdom, courage, justice, temperance, by how much attention you gave each one this week. Pick the one you ranked lowest and write one specific action for next week that practices it.
- Thinking about the last week, what went well for you?
- Have you learned anything new in the past week?
- Finish this sentence: “Last week, these things could have been better:”
- Where did most of your focus actually go, and was it worth it?
- Which day worked best, and what made it work?
- What did this week teach you about your estimates?
- What almost went wrong this week and quietly didn't?
- What did you say yes to that should have been a no?
- Which small win this week deserves to be repeated on purpose?
- What drained you this week that you could refuse next week?
- Which task did you avoid all week, and what is it really about?
- Which system broke this week, and what is its two-minute patch?
- What would you tell last Monday's you?
- What are you still pretending not to know?
- Let’s review the past week: how well did your priorities match what you did?
- Thinking about the last week or so, is there anything you would have de-prioritized?
- Thinking back on the past week, can you think of any times when you had to change your priorities? What changed to make the shift happen?
- Looking at this past week, was there a moment where you surprised yourself by doing something you weren't sure you could?
- When did you most feel rushed in the last week?
- Where did your mind keep drifting to this week, and why do you think it kept pulling you there?
- What happened this past week that left you feeling satisfied?
- From the past week or so, can you think of any mistakes you made that you could forgive yourself for?
- Can you complete this sentence: “This is something I learned last week:”
- In the last week, how many mornings began when you wanted them to?
- What did you buy, read or watch this week that was actually worth it?
- What made you laugh this week?

## priorities
- Write next week's intention in a single sentence, the way Marcus Aurelius wrote to himself each morning.
- What is the one thing within your control that you will carry into next week?
- What is one habit from this week worth keeping exactly as it is?
- What is one habit from this week worth dropping before next week starts?
- Based on the last 7 days, what would you change about the next 7 days?
- What is the single biggest open loop you carry into next week?
- If next week copied this week exactly, would that be good news?
- What did you start this week that deserves to survive next week?
- Finish the sentence: “If I could only do one thing next week, …”
- Make a list of 5 things that you won't do this week.
- Finish this sentence: “Next week, I will stop…”
- Make a to-do list (of at most 5 things) for the next week. Then remove one.
- Make a list of five things you want to prioritize for the coming week. Choose one, and remove it.
- What's one task you can delegate or drop this week?
- Thinking about the next week, can you list 3 things you want to change, in a very small, achievable way?
- In the next week or so, what can you do that would help you achieve big life goals?
- Think of one thing you want to be true in five years. Can you do anything to make it happen this week?
- Looking at the next 7 days, can you see any strategic work you need to do?
- Can you list three problems you expect to solve in the next week or so?
- In the next week or so, can you think of any ways you can reduce complexity?
- Is there anything you can move or prioritize this week to help you finish a project?
- Finish this sentence: “I know that by next week, what will be different is…”

## energy
- How do you want to spend more time next week?
- How do you want to spend less time next week?
- For the week ahead list the top three things that will need a lot of your energy.
- This week, where in your calendar can you put time in for recovery?
- What could you do next week to reserve some energy for yourself?
- In the next week, what changes could you make to feel less rushed?
- What can you change next week that will let you feel more restful?
- If you had to draw a pie chart of where your energy goes in a typical week, what would the biggest slice be?
- Make a slow-plan: when in the next week can you slow down for 10 minutes?
- What's one thing you can put in next week that you'll really look forward to and that will cost you absolutely nothing?
- In the week ahead, what can you schedule just for you to enjoy?
- How will you rest for the purpose of being more well next week?
- Can you list 4 moments you wish you could relive from the last week?
- What would have made this week 10% calmer?

## people
- Which conversation this week changed something?
- Who did you not make time for that you meant to?
- Over the past week, what role did compassion play in your actions?
- Thinking of the week ahead, can you increase the happiness of anyone else?
- How can you be joyful towards people next week?
- In the next week, can you create any comfort for someone else?
- Would you want to meet new people in the next week?
- Can you think of anyone you could thank for doing a thankless task?
- Who got the best of your attention this week, and who got the leftovers?
- Thinking about the week, whose day did you make better, and who made yours?

## open
- What's something you've been walking around for weeks, pretending it isn't there?
- Finish this sentence: “Every week, I…”
- Thinking over the last week, can you list the kinds of things you’ve consumed?
- Thinking about the past week or so, what have you made?
- Look back at the past week and try to list some glimmers that you remember.
- If you tried telling nothing but the absolute truth for a week, how do you think it would go?
- If you did not need your paycheck, what would your new normal week look like?
- Make a to-do list of only pleasurable things you could do this week.
- What deserves your slowness and attention in the coming weeks?
- What are you planning to say yes to in the next week?

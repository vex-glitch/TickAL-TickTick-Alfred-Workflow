# Evening journal pools (Vex 2026-09-24): one ## section per category, two prompts drawn per
# category per journal (periodic_model.select_prompts), a ### chain: block is asked in order on a
# chain night. Sources: the Stoic app's public library (getstoic.com), Vex's own list, regpaq,
# the Stoic canon. [person] is filled from the 👽 People list. No en or em dashes.

## review
- Where did I fall short today? Describe the moment without the excuse attached to it.
- What did I do well today? Name one specific moment, not a general feeling.
- What is one thing today that I am glad I did not react to?
- What is one thing I said or did today that I would take back if I could?
- What did I leave undone that I told myself this morning I would do?
- What will I do differently tomorrow because of what happened today?
- Close the page with one line: tomorrow I will ______.
- What bad habit did I curb today?
- What did I do badly? What did I do well? What will I try next time?
- What moment from today is worth examining?
- Was I prepared enough?
- What did you omit? Did you overlook any opportunities to exercise virtue or strength of character?
- What was the last thing that took more effort than you planned?
- When was the last time you said, “I’ll do it later”? How long was that “later”? Do you find yourself saying it often? In what situations?
- What have I learned that will help me tomorrow?
- Think about a complication you recently solved. How did you do it? Can you apply that same approach to other challenges?

## control
- Write one sentence separating the event itself from your judgment about the event.
- What happened, and what assumptions am I making about it?
- What emotion ran the day, and did I choose it or did it choose me?
- Does getting upset provide you with more options?
- When was the last time you caught yourself worrying about someone else’s choices?
- Think back to the last time something went wrong. Explain what happened in the simplest way possible. Maybe start with: “The problem was…”
- Whose opinion takes up more room in your head than it deserves?
- When you worry, what are you choosing not to see?
- How is your anger hurting you more than the thing that caused it?
- When did you last misread someone's intentions and it turned out to be wrong in a meaningful way?
- Do you feel pressure to have an opinion about everything happening around you?
- What’s a conversation or an argument you’ve had in your imagination lately? Was it a “redo” of a real interaction you’ve had? How did it go in your mind?
- Have you ever taken something personally that had nothing to do with you?
- Are you worrying about what will happen later instead of doing the right thing, right now?
- What’s a recent worst-case scenario your mind came up with? How does it affect your mood and daily choices?
- What triggers your anger lately? Is there something that gets under your skin more quickly or more often than usual?
- To what demands from the future can you happily say: “I don’t care at all”?
- Do you find that things outside of yourself cause stress, or does stress emerge from within?
- Do I rule my fears, or do they rule me?
- What habits of people close to you drive you absolutely wild? Think of something someone does repeatedly that annoys you. Why does it frustrate you so much?
- What tends to make you lose your cool?
### chain: worry sort
1. List everything on your mind right now, worry by worry, with no sorting yet.
2. From that list (shown below), name only the items that are entirely up to your own choices and actions.
3. From that list, name the items that depend on other people, chance, or an outcome you can't decide alone.
4. Pick one item from your answer to 3. Write the one thing about it that is still yours to choose: your response.
5. For the item that worries you most, write what you are actually afraid will happen if you let it go.
6. Rewrite your worry list sorted into two groups: mine to act on, mine only to accept.
7. What would it look like to act fully on the items from 2 and simply note the items from 3 without acting on them?

## virtue
- Temperance: What did I resist today that I usually give in to?
- Which of the four virtues did I neglect today, and what pulled me away from it?
- Courage: What did I do today that required courage, even in a small, private form?
- Wisdom: Where did I choose the wiser option over the easier one today?
- Justice: Where did I treat someone fairly today, even when it cost me something?
- Which of the four virtues came easiest to me today, and why?
- What did you do today because it felt like the right thing to do? You didn’t have to do it, no one made you, but you felt it was just right.
- Can you think of anything you avoid doing (regularly) because it makes you uncomfortable?
- When do you feel justified in breaking your own rules?
- Is there something you rarely say out loud because you know it’ll get pushback?
- Do you ever hold back criticism to avoid hurting someone? How often do you bite your tongue, and what makes you do it?
- Do you hold yourself to a different standard than the people around you?
- How do you talk and respond to people when you’re angry? Do your words, tone, or timing ever make things worse?
- When was the last time you did more than what was asked of you?
- When did you last object to something you didn’t want to do?
- Looking at this past week, was there a moment where you surprised yourself by doing something you weren't sure you could? What does that tell you about the gap between who you think you are and who you actually are when it counts?
- Did you see anything unfair today (or yesterday)? What was it?

## desire
- What did I crave today? Describe it in its most simplistic form. Did it warrant that level of craving?
- Is my mind being held hostage by someone else's exciting life, better skills, or bigger accomplishments? Why should I be happy for them and why should I not compare myself to them?
- What's something you've been walking around for weeks, pretending it isn't there?
- What do I really want to buy right now? What value will that add to my life? What value will be lost if I don't buy it? Is there a better use for the money I would have to spend to acquire it?
- When did you say yes to something you didn’t need at all, just because you simply wanted it?
- Have you ever done something just to get recognition or validation? When was the last time you did something mainly to be noticed or praised?
- Think of something I desperately wanted to have and eventually purchased. How do I feel about this item now? Was the craving warranted?
- Think of something I've done that I regret. Write myself an apology and my own response of forgiveness.
- What does being “behind” actually mean to you? Who or what set that timeline in the first place? What schedule are you measuring yourself against?
- Finish the sentence: “I feel empty after I…”
- Which of my possessions own me?
- Have you ever spent money to avoid something? Paying for take out to avoid cooking comes to mind…
- Is there something you do partly, or mostly, for the perks that come with it? What's the perk, and is it actually worth it?
- What’s the thing you buy most often with the least amount of thought?
- Are there any stories you hold onto that aren’t entirely accurate? Why do you keep them, and what might change if you faced the truth?
- When was the last time you wanted something you couldn’t have? Try a small thing, like a coffee you didn’t have time for.
- Finish this sentence: “I could happily live without…” What first comes to mind? Is there anything you can plan to remove from your life?
- Finish this sentence: “I already have enough…”
- Do you often judge other people’s extravagance? What drives the judgment? Do you think there might be a bit of jealousy in there?
- When was the last time you caught yourself daydreaming about a “better” version of your life? Did it inspire you, or leave you wanting more? Why do you think that is?

## connection
- Was there a time you could’ve said something nice but didn’t? Have you ever held back a compliment or positive review? Why did you stay quiet?
- Did someone annoy or upset me today? What good or harm do I think they intended? Or was it ignorance? How could I have better empathized with them and their actions in the moment?
- Is there someone you need to make amends to?
- Has someone shown you a little kindness recently?
- What did I do that was unfriendly, or antisocial, or inconsiderate?
- Can you think of anyone who might need a friendly voice right now?
- Write a thank-you letter to someone who doesn’t know you’re grateful. Maybe you didn’t get the chance to thank them properly.
- Who is the person from your life who you think about with a low-level irritation?
- Is there a relationship in your life where you feel that you're doing most of the work?
- Which conversation from your past stays with you today?
- How could you act compassionately tomorrow? Is there anything you could plan, an activity, an action to take to help someone out?

## open
- What are the non-negotiables in your life? Which boundaries or principles never get exceptions, no matter what?
- Finish the sentence: “It doesn’t matter how bad things get, I would never…”
- Think of the last time fear of failure or rejection stopped you. What would the actual consequences have been if you had tried?
- What is something you’ve intentionally placed in an unspecified future? What feels safer or more comfortable about keeping it there for now, without actively working toward it?
- Who or what benefits from your self-doubt? When you hold yourself back or stay quiet about your strengths, who (or what) gains from that?
- Where in your life is comfort keeping you in a loop?
- How much of your life is designed around the need to feel safe?
- Is there anything you don’t trust yourself about?
- Can you recall a time when letting your emotions take over was clearly a mistake? How might things have turned out if you had cooled down first?
- Make a to-do list (of at most 5 things) for the next week. Then remove one. What made you choose this one? Is this something you can choose not to do this week?
- What old patterns do you fall back into when you’re stressed?
- How would a close friend describe your priorities? How well do you think they know the answer?
- When you strip away all the perks, what are the things in your life you’d choose anyway?
- Is there a benefit or perk you’d never accept, no matter how good it looked on paper?
- Can you think of something you're supposed to be excited about that you're actually feeling neutral about?
- Can you make a list of 5 self-imposed restrictions you currently carry?
- Think of a belief you once had but later changed. What caused your shift in perspective?
- Looking back, what hardships are you most grateful for?
- Where did your mind keep drifting to this week, and why do you think it kept pulling you there?
- What’s one old habit that still follows you everywhere?
- What daily habits clutter your mental space?
- Is there something you’re deliberately taking your time with right now? Is there something you’re not ready to rush? What are you protecting by going slowly?
- Is there anything you could do tomorrow morning that you could keep doing every morning for a month? You’ll have to think small if you will be able to keep doing it.
- What's something that feels lighter or more comfortable in you now than it did a year ago? Sometimes healing is like not flinching at something that used to sting. What's your version of that?
- Finish this sentence: “Recently, I noticed, for the first time…” Try anything that stood out, or anything new. What did you notice? Try to be specific.
### chain: On Alignment
1. What is a value? Do you have examples?
2. Make a list of your 5 core values.
3. What small change could you make next week that would make you feel more aligned with your values?
### chain: On Hedonic Adaptation
1. When did you last really look forward to something good happening?
2. Can you describe a time you realized that you started to feel a bit blasé about a really good thing in your life?
3. Can you ever reignite gratitude for a good thing in your life?
### chain: On Repair
1. Have you got anything that needs repairing? Is it a thing, or a part of a thing?
2. Is there anything you wish you’d mended?
3. Can you list 3 things you can do next week that will repair something damaged?

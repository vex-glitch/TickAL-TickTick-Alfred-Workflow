## lookback
- What did you do this year that you’d never done before?
- What kept you sane?
- The wisest decision I made...
- What was your biggest personal change from January to December of this past year?
- What was the biggest risk I took this year?
- Are there any months that look particularly busy or stressful to you?

## lessons
- What’s something you changed your mind about this year?
- What are the 20% of people, behaviors, beliefs, or environments that led to 80% of the good results?
- What did I learn from any work mistakes I made?
- What was your most overrated success?
- What's a thinking bias you've become aware of this year?
- Where did I make progress that doesn’t show up in any goal I wrote down?

## people
- Who are the three people who influenced you the most?
- Who did you drift from this year?
- Can you think of three people you haven’t spoken to for a long time?
- Is there anyone who you would actually pay to never email or contact you again?
- Who did you meet, reconnect with or get closer to this year?
- What were 3 of the hardest conversations you had?

## letting go
- Is there anything you must let go of before you can start your next year?
- What are the 20% of people, behaviors, beliefs, or environments that led to 80% of the bad results?
- What did I try to manage, worry about, or resist that was never mine to determine?
- In what part of your life might you declare bankruptcy and start over?
- What project should I have quit earlier?
- Are you holding yourself to, and judging yourself by, standards of productivity or performance that are impossible to meet?

## direction
- Is there any single thing you want to change over the next 12 months?
- What do you think your biggest risk will be?
- What am I most excited to build in the upcoming year?
- What would you like to have next year that you lacked this year?
- What skill should I focus on developing in the new year?
- Where can I spend money to make life easier?

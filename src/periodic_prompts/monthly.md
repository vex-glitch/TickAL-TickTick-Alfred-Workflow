# Monthly journal pools (Vex 2026-09-24, late): one ## section per category, TWO prompts drawn from EVERY
# category each month (ten in all, periodic_model.select_prompts, MONTHLY_CATEGORIES). Sources, all
# verbatim and verified against their pages by an Opus hunt: Sahil Bloom's monthly review, RescueTime,
# Todoist, Productive Flourishing, Sarah K Peck, Avthar, Meaning Ring, Stoic of the Day's 30-day
# challenge, Stoic Wolf, Jerry Colonna, Oliver Burkeman, Massimo Pigliucci, Daily Stoic, Seneca, Marcus
# Aurelius, the Pythagorean triad, the Stoic app's month-scoped prompts, and eight of lestallion.com's
# fifty monthly prompts (Vex's find). Vex's six monthly questions are SET questions, not here. The July pool is retired.
# No chains. No en or em dashes.

## patterns
- What feedback did you receive, and how did you use it?
- What new projects or responsibilities did you take on?
- What was your intention at the start of the month? How did you actually spend your time?
- What mistakes did you make over and over again?
- What were you doing when you were achieving your best results?
- When am I the most productive? How can I create these conditions more often?
- Did you spend your time in a way that was meaningful or conscious or were you ruled by habits and defaults?
- What surprised me this month?
- Can you make a list of 3 things that happened in the past month that you wish you had expected beforehand?
- What were my biggest realizations?
- In what moments did it feel like I was working within my zone of genius?
- Look back in memory and consider when you ever had a fixed plan, how few days have passed as you had intended, when you were ever at your own disposal

## direction
- What's a new way you can increase your income?
- What am I thinking about for the month ahead?
- What goals or projects need to be adjusted or dropped?
- What projects or tasks have fallen off the radar?
- Are there projects that are weighing on you and should be delegated or discarded?
- Are my major goals still my most important goals?
- Are my current systems and habits aligned with my long-term goals?
- What do I need to cut from my life to operate more efficiently?
- What are your Big 3 for the month?
- Are there any especially busy weeks where you may need to prune either work or personal commitments?
- What kinds of experiences would you like to schedule into the upcoming month?
- What do you most want to do in the next month or so? Why do you want to do it? Will you?
- Are you hunting antelope (big important problems) or field mice (small urgent problems)?
- How will you know that you've done this thing? What will be the clue, the evidence, the metric?
- Would you want to rearrange any of your priorities to feel more satisfied?

## cost
- What were your biggest expenses?
- How much time did I spend on social media this month?
- Control audit: Where did I spend effort on the uncontrollable?
- Where did sorting what you control actually help? Note it.
- Are you sure the goals you're pursuing are what you truly desire?
- What were the risks you took?
- Where did you not try hard enough?
- What are 5 objects you could easily remove from your life?
- Where in your life or work are you currently pursuing comfort when what's called for is a little discomfort?
- In which areas of life are you still holding back until you feel like you know what you are doing?
- How often has anger been a problem?
- Which judgment, once questioned, lost its power over you?
- Where did you act from character rather than impulse?

## people
- Who did you spend the most time with this month?
- Did you have any conflicts, and how did you resolve them?
- What was the most meaningful conversation you had?
- Who do you need to thank or appreciate more?
- Who are the people I connected with this past month?
- What things may I have prioritized over my relationships? Are those things worth it?
- How good have I been at performing my duties toward others, or at engaging in self-control?
- What am I not saying that I need to say?
- How have I been complicit in creating the conditions I say I don't want?
- Were my actions just?
- What duty was left undone?
- Who do you owe the most, in the non-financial sense?
- Who has surprised you by becoming a helpful presence in your life?
- Is there a relationship in your life where you feel that you're doing most of the work?
- Can you think of any people you know who live according to their values?

## open
- What really matters right now in my life? Am I dedicating the necessary energy to it?
- What were my biggest wins & milestones?
- What areas am I most satisfied? Least satisfied?
- How do you feel about the work you're doing right now? Do you feel like you're doing your best work? Why or why not?
- What do you perceive to be lacking in your life and work right now? What can you do about it?
- When was the last time you rewarded yourself, and when will be the next?
- Did you go after what you want or did you hope it would come to you?
- Have we done the work we've needed to do?
- Make a to-do list of things you have already finished this month.
- About what am I now employing my own soul?
- One habit to subtract; one practice to deepen.

# Morning journal pools (Vex 2026-09-24): one ## section per category, two prompts drawn per
# category per journal (periodic_model.select_prompts), a ### chain: block is asked in order on a
# chain night. Sources: the Stoic app's public library (getstoic.com), Vex's own list, regpaq,
# the Stoic canon. [person] is filled from the 👽 People list. No en or em dashes.

## prepare
- What is the one obstacle most likely to throw off my day? Name it specifically, not vaguely.
- What am I dreading right now, and what would meeting it well actually look like?
- Who do I want to be by 6pm today? Name one trait, not a mood.
- What is the one thing within my control today? Write it as a single sentence.
- What is outside my control today that I am tempted to worry about anyway?
- Which virtue will I practice on purpose today: wisdom, courage, justice, or temperance?
- In one plain sentence, what is today actually going to require of me?
- Finish this sentence before you close the page: today went well if I ______.
- What response do I want to rehearse?
- What is the harder choice I'm avoiding?
- Is there an important conversation that you’ve been putting off? What feels difficult about starting it? What might change if you did?
- What’s something you've been putting off that you know probably isn't as bad as it plays in your head?
- Where might you be pushing too hard for a perfect outcome?
- What's the smallest step I can take toward a big thing today?
- What's one task you can delegate or drop this week?
- What is the most important thing you can do in the next 5 minutes?
- What will you spend the most time focusing on today?
- Make a list of 5 things that you won't do this week.

## people
- What value has [person] added to my life? What wisdom, achievements, or fortunate events of mine can I give them some credit for?
- Who do you owe the most, in the non-financial sense?
- What part of this [person's] character do I admire? If I adopted that part to my character, how would that change my actions?
- Who taught you something? Write it down. One person or one thing.
- Who is the most patient person you know? How do they handle annoying situations?
- Has anyone ever been unexpectedly honest with you in a way that actually helped?
- What value do I (or can I) add to [person's] life in return?
- Who have you copied the most in your life because you genuinely wanted to be more like them?
- What lessons or bits of wisdom from loved ones who’ve passed do you still carry with you? Who shared that with you, and do you find it still guiding you today?
- How does [person] affect and influence my life decisions, feelings, and behavior?
- Can you think of anyone you could thank for doing a thankless task? People you know, people who support you or your community, people overlooked…
- Who do you need to thank at this moment?
- Can you think of any people you know who live according to their values?
- Who has surprised you by becoming a helpful presence in your life?
- Finish the sentence: “Despite everything, I’m thankful for …”
- Who comes to mind first when you think of the words "good people"?

## perspective
- Where am I standing in my own way?
- What was the most urgent thing you spent time on yesterday? What specific things made that time urgent?
- Is there something in your normal day that you wish you could pay more attention to?
- Can you think of any aspects in your daily life that you don’t remember choosing to let in? Some things seem to creep in, like a smart-device alert or the need for a third cup of coffee…
- Is there something you've been trying to force that refuses to be forced?
- When you look back at the things you were afraid of, what did your imagination consistently get wrong? The version of an experience we build in our heads is never the real one. What does your pattern tell you?
- With every accident, ask yourself what abilities you have for making a proper use of it.
- Where in your life are you moving too fast right now?
- Make a list of 5 physical things you would miss if you lost them tomorrow.
- What routine part of your day do you usually take for granted?
- Is there anything you would find hard to give thanks for? Why?
- Can you make a list of 3 things that happened in the past month that you wish you had expected beforehand? These could be small like an untimely travel delay, or large like a big cancellation. Could you have known?
- Do you catch yourself judging people at first glance? What’s the first thing you usually notice about others? How quickly do you form an opinion?
- Is there anything in your life right now that you've been undervaluing because it didn't cost you enough?
- Have you got plenty of time? Think about your calendar: when are you free? When do you want to be free?
- When was the last time you felt like asking: “Why am I even doing this?” Did you ever answer that, by the way?
- What has going through hard things given you that you didn’t have before?
